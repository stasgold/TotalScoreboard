Privacy policy

SupportPrivacy policy

Applies to Total Scoreboard for iOS, iPadOS and Android. Last updated 8 October 2026.

In short: Total Scoreboard does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any information about you. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Information the app handles

The app stores the games, sets, counter names and scores you enter. This information is kept only in the app's storage on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking, and no third-party services or SDKs that do so.
  • No network connections: the app works fully offline.
  • No access to your contacts, location, photos, camera, microphone or other personal data.

Backups

Like other app data, your scores may be included in the backups your device makes, for example iCloud Backup on iPhone and iPad or Android backup to your Google account. These backups are handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies and your device settings; we have no access to them.

Sharing

When you use Share, the app hands plain text of your scores to the app you choose (for example Messages or Mail). Nothing is sent unless you do this yourself.

Deleting your data

Delete a game inside the app, or delete the app to remove all of its data from your device.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
