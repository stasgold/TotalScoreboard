Keep score for any game: card games, board games, darts, mini golf, table tennis, or anything else you count. Available for iPhone, iPad and Android.

Email stasgold@gmail.com. Please mention your device and what you were doing; we usually reply within a few days.

Quick guide

  • Create a game with the + button and choose how many points a tap adds.
  • Add counters, one for each player or team.
  • Tap a card to add points, long-press to subtract. Tap the ± label on a card to add any amount; type a minus sign to subtract.
  • Sets are the tabs of a game: rounds, legs, holes or matches. A new set can copy the players of the current one with every score at 0.
  • With two or more sets, the Total tab adds everyone up and crowns the leader. Turn on Lowest score wins in the game's settings for golf, Hearts and similar games.
  • Undo (iPhone and iPad) takes back the last score change.
  • Share sends the scores as plain text to a chat, an email or your notes.

Questions

Where are my scores stored?
Only on your device. They are included in your normal device backups, so they come back when you restore a backup or move to a new phone.

How do I delete my data?
Delete a game from the games list, or delete the app to remove everything.

Does the app need an account or an internet connection?
No. It works fully offline and has no accounts, ads or tracking.
